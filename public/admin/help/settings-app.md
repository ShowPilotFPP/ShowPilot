# Settings › Install as App

Lets people add ShowPilot to their home screen as an app that opens full-screen, without the browser's address bar. You can turn this on separately for your admin pages and for your viewer page.

This is about installing your own pages as a home-screen app. Listing your show in the ShowPilot phone app is on [Settings › ShowPilot App](help:settings-showpilot-app).

## Admin app

Tick **Enable Install as App for admin** to let you and other admins install the admin pages as an app. It's called **ShowPilot Admin** and uses the ShowPilot icon; neither can be changed. Off by default.

After turning it on, reload the page so your browser notices. Then:

- **Chrome or Edge on a computer, and Android:** the browser offers to install, often with a small icon in the address bar. You can also use the **Install ShowPilot Admin** button.
- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. On these devices the button reads **How to install on iOS** and shows the steps.

The button changes to **Already installed** when you're using the installed app. If your browser isn't ready to install, tapping the button shows a short report of what it found and the likely reason, for example that the app is already installed, that you dismissed an install offer recently, or that the browser doesn't support installing apps.

> **Tip:** Installing the admin on a phone or tablet makes it quick to reach during the show. For a big-button remote, also look at the [Cockpit](help:cockpit).

## Viewer app

Tick **Enable Install as App for viewer** to let viewers install your show page as an app. Off by default.

When it's on, browsers that support it show a small **Install app** button in the bottom-left corner of your viewer page. Viewers can tap × to hide it; it then stays hidden on that phone. On iPhone and iPad, viewers use Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

### App name

The name viewers see under the icon on their home screen. You can use up to 60 characters. Leave it blank to use your **Show name** from [Settings › General](help:settings-general#show-name).

Keep it short. Where space is tight, only the first 12 characters are used, and phones cut long names off.

### App icon

The picture viewers see on their home screen and on the **Install app** button. Choose a PNG or JPEG file; it saves as soon as you pick it, and a preview appears below. **Clear** removes it.

Use a square image, 512×512 pixels or larger. The file must be under 250 KB, and a 512×512 PNG usually is. Without an icon, viewers get the ShowPilot icon.

> **Tip:** Phones may crop the corners of the icon into a circle or rounded square, so keep important details away from the edges.
